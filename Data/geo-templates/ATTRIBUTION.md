are computed from the DEM. No OSM, WorldCover, bathymetry, or other datasets are used.
Copernicus DEM is a surface model and can include vegetation and buildings.
These outputs are for fictional game terrain, not navigation.

WorldCover、水深或其他資料集。Copernicus DEM 為地表模型，可能包含植被與建築。
本資料僅供虛構遊戲地形使用，不得用於真實航行導航。
